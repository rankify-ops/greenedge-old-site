Heat Pump Hot Water Heater

Top Domestic Hot Water Heat Pumps in Australia have seen significant advancements over the past 30 years. However, more than 75% of brands in the market still rely on outdated synthetic refrigerants facing bans, employ obsolete technology, offer inadequate warranties, and lack reliable service networks. Many of these systems will struggle to last beyond 3-4 years.

Establishing a certification authority is essential to weed out substandard products, protecting consumers from misleading claims and exploitation. Clarity regarding rebates is also crucial; some companies advertise "FREE" installations supported by government rebates, yet customers often encounter hidden costs due to undisclosed conditions.

GreenEdge applies our own certification to systems for your project, provides rebate calculation documents, and connects you with fully accredited installers knowledgeable about the product. They offer comprehensive quotations and manage all rebate processes. If you're also installing a Hydronic System, GreenEdge determines whether integrating domestic hot water into an "All in One Hydronic & Hot Water System" or keeping it independent is optimal. Some of these heat pumps suit OFF GRID applications.
